# Lesson Experience Design

## Lesson length
Target 5–10 minutes for a standard lesson, with optional extension practice.

## Lesson sequence
1. **Context:** introduce a realistic situation, image, or short dialogue.
2. **Listen:** play a short recording and check overall understanding.
3. **Notice:** present useful expressions, meaning, and simple pronunciation support.
4. **Practice:** use varied recognition and production activities.
5. **Read and write:** work with a short, level-appropriate text and a meaningful response.
6. **Speak:** invite the learner to respond or record speech; recording is optional and consent-based.
7. **Interact:** provide a short role-play or conversation task.
8. **Feedback:** explain important errors, show a better form, and allow another attempt.
9. **Review:** schedule selected items for spaced retrieval.
10. **Check:** assess the lesson objective with a short performance task.

## Activity principles
- Every activity must support a stated learning objective.
- Vary interaction: listening selection, matching, ordering, typing, reading, dialogue, and spoken response.
- Avoid excessive points, streak pressure, or repetitive drills.
- Give clear instructions and accessible alternatives.
- Do not penalize learners for accent differences when the target is intelligibility and the pronunciation is acceptable.

## Feedback
Feedback should identify what worked, explain one or two high-value improvements, provide a model, and invite retry. Distinguish spelling, grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, and pronunciation feedback.

## Accessibility and mobile use
- Design for small screens and touch interaction.
- Use readable typography, adequate contrast, captions, and transcripts.
- Do not rely on color or audio alone to convey meaning.
- Support interrupted sessions and preserve progress safely.
