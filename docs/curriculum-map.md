# Curriculum Map

This is the initial scope map. Detailed lesson objectives, vocabulary, grammar scope, and assessment rubrics will be authored and reviewed before publication.

## Pre-A1 — First contact with Arabic
- Direction of writing and basic script awareness
- Letter shapes and high-value sound contrasts
- Greetings and polite expressions
- Names, countries, languages, and simple introductions
- Numbers and familiar classroom phrases
- Reading short labels and writing basic personal information

## A1 — Everyday foundations
- Introductions and personal details
- Family and relationships
- Home and daily objects
- Food, ordering, and preferences
- Time, dates, and daily routines
- Places, directions, transport, and simple shopping

## A2 — Independent everyday communication
- Work and study
- Travel and accommodation
- Health and appointments
- Plans, invitations, and arrangements
- Past events and future intentions
- Short messages, forms, and practical descriptions

## B1 — Connected communication
- Personal stories and experiences
- Explaining problems and solutions
- Opinions, reasons, and comparisons
- Community, services, and everyday media
- Longer conversations and connected paragraphs

## B2 — Confident communication
- Contemporary issues and media
- Discussion and debate
- Workplace communication and presentations
- Evaluating claims and supporting opinions
- More complex texts and extended writing

## C1 — Advanced academic and professional use
- Reports, proposals, and formal correspondence
- Academic discussion and structured argument
- Nuance, register, and audience awareness
- Synthesizing information from multiple sources

## C2 — Precision and flexibility
- Fine distinctions in meaning and style
- Complex spontaneous discussion
- High-level writing and editing
- Context-sensitive register and rhetorical choices

## Required lesson metadata
Each published lesson must include: level, unit, objective, prerequisites, target expressions, vocabulary, language focus, skill focus, media provenance, activity definitions, answer keys or rubrics, accessibility notes, and review schedule.
