# Academic Learning Model

## Mission
Teach learners to understand and use contemporary, widely understood Arabic in real situations. The initial audience is English-speaking beginners, with a mobile-first learning experience.

## Language policy
- Core instruction uses contemporary standard Arabic that is broadly understood across Arabic-speaking regions.
- Do not make a regional dialect the default foundation.
- Label regional variants explicitly when they are introduced.
- Beginner Arabic is normally displayed without vowel marks; add pronunciation support in simple Latin letters and audio.
- Explain new language in clear English at the beginning, then gradually increase Arabic exposure.

## Proficiency progression
Use CEFR as a planning reference, not as a claim of external accreditation.

| Stage | Communicative outcomes |
|---|---|
| Pre-A1 | Recognize basic sounds and letters; greet, identify objects, exchange simple personal information. |
| A1 | Handle familiar introductions, family, home, food, time, routines, places, directions, and simple purchases. |
| A2 | Communicate about work, study, travel, health, plans, past events, and simple messages. |
| B1 | Narrate experiences, explain problems, express opinions, and understand common everyday texts. |
| B2 | Discuss contemporary topics, compare viewpoints, and communicate in common professional situations. |
| C1 | Produce clear academic and professional communication, reports, and structured arguments. |
| C2 | Communicate with high precision, flexibility, and sensitivity to nuance. |

## Four integrated skills
Every level develops listening, speaking, reading, and writing. A lesson may emphasize one skill, but should connect it to at least one other skill.

## Assessment principles
- Assess what learners can understand and do, not only what they can memorize.
- Use short formative checks throughout lessons.
- Include periodic performance tasks and level reviews.
- Give actionable feedback and a chance to retry.
- Track confidence separately from demonstrated proficiency.

## Content integrity
Create original lessons and media or use properly licensed materials. Record provenance and license information for imported assets. Do not reproduce protected textbook content.
